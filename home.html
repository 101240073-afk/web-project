About WORK LINK

Building connections that help communities recover and grow.

WORK LINK is a platform designed to connect skilled independent workers with organizations involved in rebuilding and recovery projects.

Our goal is to simplify the process of finding skilled labor, managing construction tasks, and supporting communities through organized collaboration.

How It Works

A simple process that connects skilled workers with rebuilding opportunities.

01

Project Managers Post Tasks

Organizations and project managers publish rebuilding tasks, including the required skills, location, and deadlines.

02

Workers Apply

Skilled workers create professional profiles, browse available opportunities, and apply for tasks that match their experience.

03

Managers Review & Manage

Project managers review applications, select suitable workers, organize tasks, and monitor project progress.

04

Communities Rebuild

Workers and organizations collaborate to complete projects and support the recovery of local communities.
